# Therapist.Me Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Introduction

Therapist.Me ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## HIPAA Compliance

Therapist.Me is designed to comply with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy and Security Rules. We treat all health information you provide as Protected Health Information (PHI).

### Your Rights Under HIPAA

You have the right to:

1. **Access Your Information**: Request copies of your health information at any time
2. **Request Corrections**: Ask us to correct health information you believe is incorrect
3. **Request Restrictions**: Ask us to limit how we use or share your information
4. **Request Confidential Communications**: Ask us to contact you in a specific way
5. **Request an Accounting**: Get a list of certain disclosures we've made
6. **File a Complaint**: If you believe your privacy rights have been violated

## Information We Collect

### Personal Health Information (PHI)

We collect and store the following PHI:

- **Recovery Information**: Substance use history, sobriety dates, recovery goals
- **Mental Health Data**: Mood entries, emotional states, stress levels
- **Journal Entries**: Personal reflections, thoughts, and experiences
- **Progress Data**: Milestones, achievements, streaks, statistics
- **Chat Conversations**: Messages with the virtual therapist
- **Exercise Completions**: Therapy exercises and wellness activities completed

### Personal Information

- **Account Information**: Username, email address (optional)
- **Profile Information**: First name (optional), age, gender (optional)
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifier
- **Usage Data**: App features used, session duration, interaction patterns

### Information You Provide

- **Emergency Contacts**: Names and phone numbers of emergency contacts
- **Goals and Preferences**: Recovery goals, notification settings
- **Forum Posts**: Anonymous contributions to the peer support forum

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide Services**: Deliver personalized recovery support and mental health resources
2. **Personalize Experience**: Customize recommendations and content based on your needs
3. **Track Progress**: Monitor your recovery journey and celebrate milestones
4. **Improve Services**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app features (anonymized data only)
5. **Communicate**: Send notifications, reminders, and motivational messages (if enabled)
6. **Ensure Safety**: Monitor for crisis situations and provide appropriate resources
7. **Comply with Law**: Meet legal obligations and protect rights

## Data Security

### Encryption

- **AES-256 Encryption**: All PHI is encrypted at rest using military-grade encryption
- **In-Transit Encryption**: Data transmitted to/from our servers uses TLS 1.3
- **Secure Key Management**: Encryption keys are stored in iOS Keychain/Secure Enclave

### Access Controls

- **Biometric Authentication**: Face ID/Touch ID required to access the app
- **Session Timeout**: Automatic lockout after 15 minutes of inactivity
- **User-Specific Encryption**: Each user's data is encrypted with unique keys

### Audit Logging

- **Tamper-Proof Logs**: All access to PHI is logged with cryptographic integrity
- **Comprehensive Tracking**: Who accessed what data, when, and why
- **Regular Review**: Logs are reviewed for unauthorized access attempts

### Physical Security

- **Secure Data Centers**: Cloud storage in HIPAA-compliant facilities
- **Backup Encryption**: All backups are encrypted with the same standards
- **Disaster Recovery**: Regular backups with geographic redundancy

## Data Storage and Retention

### Local Storage

- Primary data storage on your device in encrypted format
- Deleted when you uninstall the app or delete your account

### Cloud Backup (Optional)

- Encrypted backup to iCloud (if enabled by user)
- Only you can decrypt your cloud-stored data
- Can be disabled in settings

### Retention Period

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Data deleted after 12 months of inactivity (with prior notice)
- **Deleted Accounts**: All data permanently deleted within 30 days

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information to third parties.

### Limited Exceptions

We may disclose information only in these specific circumstances:

1. **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing
2. **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or legal process
3. **Emergency Situations**: To prevent serious harm to you or others
4. **Service Providers**: With HIPAA-compliant vendors (with Business Associate Agreements)

### Anonymous Data

We may use and share anonymized, de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you for:
- Research and analytics
- Improving services
- Public health reporting

## Third-Party Services

### Analytics (Optional)

With your consent, we may use privacy-focused analytics to improve the app:
- No personally identifiable information shared
- Aggregated usage statistics only
- Can be disabled in settings

### Cloud Infrastructure

We use HIPAA-compliant cloud services:
- AWS/Google Cloud with signed Business Associate Agreements
- Data encrypted at rest and in transit
- Located in the United States

## Children's Privacy

Therapist.Me is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

## Your Choices and Controls

- **View Data**: Access all your data through the app
- **Export Data**: Download a copy of your information
- **Delete Data**: Permanently delete specific entries or your entire account
- **Update Information**: Modify your profile and settings anytime

### Communication Preferences

- **Notifications**: Control which notifications you receive
- **Frequency**: Set reminder schedules

### Privacy Settings

## Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your PHI:

1. We will notify you within 60 days of discovery
2. Notification will include:
   - What information was involved
   - Steps we're taking to investigate
   - What you can do to protect yourself
3. We will notify appropriate authorities as required by law
